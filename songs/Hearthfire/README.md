# Hearthfire
## By Sassafrass and Ada Palmer
## A song about the danger, and the necessity of dangerous things.

This has been sung at several solstices. It's suitable for Darkness. 

[Audio](https://sassafrass.bandcamp.com/album/hearthfire-single) [Author's commentary + lyrics](http://www.sassafrassmusic.com/songs/norse-mythology/hearthfire/)

Some of the lines may sound out of tune - they're not, they're just in a tune that's difficult to remember and may need some practicing. 

This song has a harmony with different words and tune sung simultaneously. For a solstice, I'd recommend either showing the two parts side-by-side, or just only putting one part up at a time and having performers or people familiar enough with the song handle the harmony.

Sheet music can be purchased from the author. She might consent to putting it here but no one has asked her yet.
